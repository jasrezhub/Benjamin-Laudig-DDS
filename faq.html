General & Appointments

Most patients benefit from a dental exam and professional cleaning every six months. However, some patients — especially those with gum disease, a history of cavities, or certain medical conditions — may need to come in more frequently. Dr. Laudig will recommend a schedule tailored to your individual oral health needs.
Yes! We warmly welcome new patients of all ages. You can book your first appointment online through our scheduling system, or call our office directly. We'll ask you to complete new patient forms before your visit, which you can find on our New Patient page.
Please bring: a valid photo ID, your dental insurance card (if applicable), a list of current medications and any known allergies, and your completed new patient forms (available on our website). Arriving 10–15 minutes early is always appreciated. If you have recent dental X-rays from a previous provider, we'd love to have those transferred too.
We do our best to accommodate same-day appointments, especially for dental emergencies. We reserve time in our schedule each day for urgent needs. Call us first thing in the morning if you're experiencing pain or a dental emergency — we'll do everything we can to see you that day.
We ask that you give us at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This allows us to offer the time slot to another patient in need. Repeated no-shows or late cancellations without notice may result in a cancellation fee. We understand that life happens — just give us a call as soon as you know.

Dental Procedures

Yes. We use modern digital X-ray technology, which emits up to 80% less radiation than traditional film X-rays. The exposure is extremely low — comparable to the radiation you receive during a short airplane flight. X-rays are an essential diagnostic tool that helps us detect problems that aren't visible to the naked eye, such as cavities between teeth, bone loss, and infections.
Modern root canal therapy is far more comfortable than its reputation suggests! With today's anesthetics and techniques, most patients report that a root canal feels similar to getting a filling. The goal is to relieve the pain caused by an infected tooth — so most people feel significantly better after the procedure. Dr. Laudig takes every measure to ensure your comfort throughout the process.
Dental implants are designed to be a permanent, lifelong solution. With proper care — brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups — implants can last 20 years or more, and many last a lifetime. The implant post (which fuses with your jawbone) is extremely durable. The crown on top may need to be replaced after 10–15 years due to normal wear.
Professional whitening results typically last 1–3 years, depending on your diet, oral hygiene habits, and lifestyle factors (such as coffee, tea, wine, or tobacco use). Touch-up treatments every 6–12 months can help maintain your bright smile. We offer both in-office whitening for immediate results and take-home trays for convenient, gradual whitening.
Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners to gradually straighten your teeth — without metal wires or brackets. They're removable, comfortable, and virtually invisible. Invisalign is effective for a wide range of alignment issues including crowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. The best way to determine candidacy is with a consultation — Dr. Laudig will evaluate your teeth and discuss whether Invisalign or another option is best for you.

Children & Family

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children have their first dental visit by their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth appearing — whichever comes first. Early visits help establish a positive relationship with dental care, allow us to monitor development, and give parents guidance on proper oral hygiene for their little ones.
Dental anxiety is completely normal, especially in children (and many adults!). Our entire team is trained in compassionate, patient-centered care. We use a "tell-show-do" approach to help kids (and nervous adults) understand what to expect at each step. We never rush, always explain what we're doing, and take breaks whenever needed. For patients with significant anxiety, we can also discuss sedation options.

Insurance & Payment

We accept most major dental insurance plans. Please visit our Insurance & Financing page for a detailed list. We recommend calling our office or your insurance provider to confirm coverage before your visit. Our team is happy to help verify your benefits and explain your coverage in plain language.
No insurance? No problem. We offer flexible payment options and accept CareCredit, allowing you to finance dental care with low or no-interest payment plans. We believe everyone deserves access to quality dental care, and our team will work with you to find a payment solution that fits your budget. Ask about our in-office savings plan as well.
Yes! We partner with CareCredit to offer flexible, low-interest and interest-free payment plans for qualifying patients. This allows you to receive the dental care you need now and pay over time in manageable monthly installments. Applying is quick and easy — our team can walk you through it at the office or you can apply online before your visit.
