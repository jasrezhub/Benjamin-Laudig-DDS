What We Provide

Our Dental Services

Comprehensive care for every smile, every age, every need — all under one roof.

🦷 Preventive Dentistry

The foundation of a healthy smile.

🧼

Routine Cleanings & Exams

Professional cleanings every 6 months to remove plaque and tartar, paired with comprehensive exams to catch issues early.

📷

Digital X-Rays

Low-radiation digital radiographs that give us a detailed view of your teeth and bone health with 80% less radiation than traditional film.

🛡️

Dental Sealants

Thin protective coatings applied to chewing surfaces to prevent cavities, especially recommended for children.

💧

Fluoride Treatment

Professional fluoride application to strengthen enamel and protect against decay for patients of all ages.

😴

Night Guards (Bruxism)

Custom-fitted night guards to protect teeth from grinding and clenching during sleep.

🔍

Oral Cancer Screening

Thorough examination of oral tissues during every routine visit to detect early signs of oral cancer.

✨ Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile and your confidence.

⚪

Professional Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening delivers dramatic results in a single visit, or take-home trays for gradual brightening.

💎

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin ceramic shells permanently bonded to teeth to correct chips, stains, gaps, and shape.

🖌️

Dental Bonding

Tooth-colored resin to repair chips, cracks, or discoloration — often completed in one visit.

😁

Smile Makeovers

A personalized combination of cosmetic procedures designed to achieve your ideal smile.

🔬

Gum Contouring

Reshaping uneven or excessive gum tissue to create a more balanced, aesthetically pleasing smile line.

🦷

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Mercury-free composite resin fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

🔧 Restorative Dentistry

Restore the health, function, and beauty of damaged or missing teeth.

👑

Dental Crowns

Custom porcelain or ceramic caps that cover and protect a damaged tooth, restoring its shape and strength.

🌉

Dental Bridges

Fixed prosthetics that fill the gap left by missing teeth, anchored to adjacent teeth or implants.

🪛

Dental Implants

The gold standard for tooth replacement — titanium posts surgically placed in the jaw to support crowns or bridges.

🦴

Dentures & Partials

Custom full or partial dentures to replace multiple missing teeth, crafted for comfort and a natural appearance.

🌡️

Root Canal Therapy

Modern root canal treatment is virtually painless and saves severely infected teeth from extraction.

✂️

Tooth Extractions

Simple and surgical extractions performed gently, including wisdom tooth removal.

😁 Orthodontics & Alignment

🔲

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Removable, virtually invisible trays that gradually straighten your teeth — no wires, no brackets.

🏋️

Retainers

Custom retainers to maintain your smile after orthodontic treatment — fixed and removable options available.

🚨 Emergency Dentistry

😣

Toothache Relief

Severe or persistent tooth pain requires prompt attention. We'll diagnose the cause and provide fast relief.

💔

Chipped or Broken Teeth

We repair chipped, cracked, or broken teeth with bonding, crowns, or other restorations — often in one visit.

🦷

Knocked-Out Tooth

Time is critical — call us immediately. Keep the tooth moist and come in as fast as possible.

🔩

Lost Fillings or Crowns

A lost restoration leaves your tooth vulnerable. We'll replace it quickly to protect the underlying tooth.

👦 Children's Dentistry

👶

First Dental Visits

We recommend first visits by age 1. Our gentle, friendly team makes it fun and stress-free.

🏅

Cleanings & Fluoride for Kids

Age-appropriate cleanings and fluoride treatments tailored to your child's needs and comfort level.

⚽

Athletic Mouthguards

Custom-fitted mouthguards for young athletes providing superior protection.

📐

Space Maintainers

When a baby tooth is lost early, space maintainers keep room open for the permanent tooth to emerge properly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our office, procedures, insurance, and more.

General & Appointments

Most patients benefit from a dental exam and professional cleaning every six months. However, some patients — especially those with gum disease, a history of cavities, or certain medical conditions — may need to come in more frequently. Dr. Laudig will recommend a schedule tailored to your individual needs.
Yes! We warmly welcome new patients of all ages. You can book your first appointment online or call our office directly. We'll ask you to complete new patient forms before your visit.
Please bring: a valid photo ID, your dental insurance card (if applicable), a list of current medications and any known allergies, and your completed new patient forms. Arriving 10–15 minutes early is always appreciated.
We do our best to accommodate same-day appointments, especially for dental emergencies. We reserve time in our schedule each day for urgent needs. Call us first thing in the morning if you're experiencing pain or a dental emergency.
We ask that you give us at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. This allows us to offer the time slot to another patient in need. We understand that life happens — just give us a call as soon as you know.

Dental Procedures

Yes. We use modern digital X-ray technology, which emits up to 80% less radiation than traditional film X-rays. The exposure is extremely low — comparable to the radiation you receive during a short airplane flight.
Modern root canal therapy is far more comfortable than its reputation suggests! With today's anesthetics and techniques, most patients report that a root canal feels similar to getting a filling. The goal is to relieve the pain caused by an infected tooth.
Dental implants are designed to be a permanent, lifelong solution. With proper care — brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups — implants can last 20 years or more, and many last a lifetime.
Professional whitening results typically last 1–3 years, depending on your diet, oral hygiene habits, and lifestyle factors. Touch-up treatments every 6–12 months can help maintain your bright smile.
Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners to gradually straighten your teeth — without metal wires or brackets. They're removable, comfortable, and virtually invisible. The best way to determine candidacy is with a consultation with Dr. Laudig.

What to Expect

Your First Appointment

Your first visit is typically about 60–90 minutes. Here's what we'll cover:

→
Review of your health history — we'll discuss your medical background, medications, and any dental concerns.
→
Comprehensive oral exam — Dr. Laudig will examine your teeth, gums, jaw, and soft tissues.
→
Digital X-rays — low-radiation images to get a complete picture of your oral health.
→
Professional cleaning — unless additional treatment is needed first.
→
Treatment planning — we'll discuss findings and recommend next steps, with no pressure.
