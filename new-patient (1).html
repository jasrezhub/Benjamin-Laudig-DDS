What to Expect

Your First Appointment

Your first visit is typically scheduled for about 60–90 minutes. Here's what we'll cover:

→
Review of your health history — we'll discuss your medical background, current medications, and any dental concerns.
→
Comprehensive oral exam — Dr. Laudig will examine your teeth, gums, jaw, and soft tissues.
→
Digital X-rays — low-radiation images to get a complete picture of your oral health.
→
Professional cleaning — unless additional treatment is needed first.
→
Treatment planning — we'll discuss findings and recommend next steps, with no pressure.
