What We Provide

Our Dental Services

Comprehensive care for every smile, every age, every need — all under one roof.

🦷 Preventive Dentistry

The foundation of a healthy smile. Prevention is always easier — and less costly — than treatment.

🧼

Routine Cleanings & Exams

Professional cleanings every 6 months to remove plaque and tartar, paired with comprehensive exams to catch issues early.

📷

Digital X-Rays

Low-radiation digital radiographs that give us a detailed view of your teeth and bone health with 80% less radiation than traditional film.

🛡️

Dental Sealants

Thin protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to prevent cavities, especially recommended for children.

💧

Fluoride Treatment

Professional fluoride application to strengthen enamel and protect against decay for patients of all ages.

😴

Night Guards (Bruxism)

Custom-fitted night guards to protect teeth from grinding and clenching during sleep, relieving jaw pain and preventing wear.

🔍

Oral Cancer Screening

Thorough examination of oral tissues during every routine visit to detect early signs of oral cancer for the best possible outcomes.

✨ Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile and your confidence with our full range of cosmetic treatments.

⚪

Professional Teeth Whitening

In-office whitening delivers dramatic results in a single visit, or take-home trays for a gradual, convenient brightening at your own pace.

💎

Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin shells of ceramic permanently bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, stains, gaps, and shape — a complete smile transformation.

🖌️

Dental Bonding

Tooth-colored resin applied and shaped to repair chips, cracks, or discoloration — a quick, affordable cosmetic fix often completed in one visit.

😁

Smile Makeovers

A personalized combination of cosmetic procedures designed to achieve your ideal smile — we work with you to plan every detail.

🔬

Gum Contouring

Reshaping uneven or excessive gum tissue to create a more balanced, aesthetically pleasing smile line.

🦷

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Mercury-free composite resin fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth — strong, safe, and invisible.

🔧 Restorative Dentistry

Restore the health, function, and beauty of damaged or missing teeth.

👑

Dental Crowns

Custom porcelain or ceramic caps that cover and protect a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance.

🌉

Dental Bridges

Fixed prosthetics that fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth, anchored to adjacent teeth or implants for a natural look and feel.

🪛

Dental Implants

The gold standard for tooth replacement — titanium posts surgically placed in the jaw to support crowns, bridges, or dentures that look and function like natural teeth.

🦴

Dentures & Partials

Custom full or partial dentures to replace multiple missing teeth, crafted for comfort, function, and a natural appearance.

🌡️

Root Canal Therapy

Modern root canal treatment is virtually painless and saves severely infected or damaged teeth from extraction, with quick recovery times.

✂️

Tooth Extractions

Simple and surgical extractions performed gently when a tooth cannot be saved, including wisdom tooth removal.

😁 Orthodontics & Alignment

Straighter teeth and a more confident smile for teens and adults.

🔲

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

The world's most popular clear aligner system. Removable, virtually invisible trays that gradually straighten your teeth — no wires, no brackets.

🏋️

Retainers

Custom retainers to maintain your smile after orthodontic treatment — both fixed and removable options available.

🚨 Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies don't wait — neither do we. Call us first.

😣

Toothache Relief

Severe or persistent tooth pain requires prompt attention. We'll diagnose the cause and provide fast, effective relief.

💔

Chipped or Broken Teeth

We repair chipped, cracked, or broken teeth with bonding, crowns, or other restorations — often in a single visit.

🦷

Knocked-Out Tooth

Time is critical — call us immediately. Keep the tooth moist and come in as fast as possible for the best chance of saving it.

🔩

Lost Fillings or Crowns

A lost restoration leaves your tooth vulnerable. We'll replace it quickly and ensure the underlying tooth is protected.

👦 Children's Dentistry

Building healthy habits and positive dental experiences from the very first visit.

👶

First Dental Visits

We recommend first visits by age 1 or within 6 months of the first tooth. Our gentle, friendly team makes it fun and stress-free.

🏅

Cleanings & Fluoride for Kids

Age-appropriate cleanings and fluoride treatments tailored to your child's needs and comfort level.

⚽

Athletic Mouthguards

Custom-fitted mouthguards for young athletes that provide superior protection compared to over-the-counter options.

📐

Space Maintainers

When a baby tooth is lost early, space maintainers keep room open for the permanent tooth to emerge properly.

Have Questions About a Specific Service?

We're happy to answer any questions and help you find the right treatment plan. Consultations are always welcome.
