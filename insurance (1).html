Insurance & Financing

We believe cost should never be a barrier to excellent dental care. We work with most major insurance providers and offer flexible financing solutions.

Insurance

We Accept Most Major Plans

Our office works with a wide range of dental insurance providers. We are in-network with many plans, which means lower out-of-pocket costs for you. If we are out-of-network with your plan, we will still file claims on your behalf and help you maximize your benefits.

Not sure if we accept your insurance? Call our friendly front desk team — we're happy to verify your benefits before your visit so there are no surprises.

We file insurance claims on your behalf
We verify your coverage before treatment
We explain your benefits in plain language
In-network & out-of-network plans accepted

Quick Insurance Tip

Most dental insurance plans cover 100% of preventive care (cleanings, exams, X-rays). This means your routine visits are often completely covered! Make sure to use your benefits — they reset each year and don't roll over.
